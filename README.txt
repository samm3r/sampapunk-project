SAMPAPUNK — play locally
========================

Best (full sound, music loops seamlessly):
  Mac/Linux: double-click start-mac-linux.command (or run: python3 -m http.server 8000)
  Windows:   double-click start-windows.bat
  Then open http://localhost:8000 . Keep the terminal window open while playing.
  Needs Python 3 (preinstalled on Mac/Linux; get it from python.org on Windows).
  Alternative: npx serve .   (Node.js)

Quick (no server): double-click index.html.
  Sound still works — the soundtrack plays through a simpler player.

Sound tips:
  - Browsers start audio only after your first click/tap/key press. That's normal.
  - Check the Music/SFX toggles in Settings and your device volume.
  - Fonts Orbitron/Rajdhani load from Google Fonts; offline, the game falls back to system fonts.
