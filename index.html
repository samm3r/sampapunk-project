HOW TO PLAY

SPACEUPJump — hold for a higher jump
DOWNSlide on foot / duck on the motorcycle. In the air: drop fast
SHIFTOverdrive boost when the bar is full
ESCPPause / resume
MMute / unmute audio
ENTERConfirm in menus
  • Touch controls. Bottom-right buttons jump (hold for higher) and slide or duck (hold). Bottom-left fires the boost. The small button above it pauses.
  • Reach the drop point. 2,800 m across six districts — on foot, then on a motorcycle, twice. This is the mission.
  • Optional cargo. Run or ride over marked boxes to stack them on your backpack (max 5). They score only when you pass a cyan DROP-OFF checkpoint: +100 per package. Every hit knocks one package off.
  • Watch for warnings. Amber dashed lines mean a drone beam is coming: low beam: jump. high beam: slide or duck. A red >>> on the left means a police interceptor is coming from behind — jump it.
  • Integrity. Every hit costs integrity. At zero, ARGOS security takes the package. Grab a pizza slice (+20) or a guaraná (+10) to repair.
  • Data chips add score and charge your boost. A full boost gives 2.6 s of overdrive: extra speed, smash through obstacles, and jam drone attacks.
  • Alert level rises over time and with every hit, from SURVEILLANCE to INTERCEPTION to LOCKDOWN.
